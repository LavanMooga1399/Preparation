The cosmic vocabulary & hierarchy

Prelims loves definition-swaps — calling a galaxy a constellation, or a light-year a unit of time. Lock the hierarchy and the definitions first.

Core definitions — the words examiners swap
TermWhat it isAnchor example / trap
GalaxyGravitationally-bound system of billions of stars, gas & dust.Milky Way (our own); Andromeda (nearest large).
StarSelf-luminous — makes own light/heat via nuclear fusion.The Sun = nearest star.
NebulaCloud of gas (hydrogen) & dust — the nursery of stars.Where every star is born.
ConstellationApparent pattern of stars as seen from Earth — not physically bound.Saptarishi (Ursa Major), Orion.
Light YearUnit of DISTANCE (~9.46 × 10¹² km) — not time.Classic UPSC trap.

⚠
Trap alert: If an option calls the light-year a unit of time, it is wrong — it measures distance. Chant once: "Light-year is distance, not time."
03

The Big Bang Theory

The most widely accepted explanation of cosmic origin — also called the Expanding Universe Hypothesis. Idea of a "primeval atom" first floated by Georges Lemaître; observational backbone supplied by Edwin Hubble.

The stages, in sequence

Big Bang — chronological stages (examiners reward the order)
StageWhenWhat happens
1 · Singularityt = 0All matter in one "tiny ball" (singular atom): unimaginably small volume, infinite density, infinite temperature.
2 · Big Bang~13.7 byaThe tiny ball explodes violently → huge, very rapid expansion; some energy converts into matter; expansion later slows.
3 · First atomsFirst 3 minutesCooling particle-soup forms the first atoms — hydrogen, then helium.
4 · TransparencyWithin 3,00,000 yrsTemperature falls to ~4,500 K → atomic matter forms; universe becomes transparent (origin of CMB).
5 · StructureHundreds of MyrGravity pulls matter into galaxies; inside them, nebulae collapse into stars.
🧠
Memory hook "1–3–3": 1 tiny ball → Bang at 13.7 bn yrs → 3 minutes (atoms) → 3,00,000 years (transparent, 4,500 K).

05

The expanding universe & Hubble

Edwin Hubble (1920s) proved the universe is expanding: galaxies drift ever farther apart, and the distance between them keeps growing. The crucial, frequently-tested nuance is what expands.

⚠
Statement check: "Galaxies are expanding" → FALSE. Correct phrasing: "the space between galaxies is increasing." This one distinction has decided prelims marks.
06

How stars & planets formed

Once matter existed, gravity organised it. The same condensation that lit up the stars also gathered leftover material into planets — this is your bridge into the origin of the solar system.

◆ Mentor bridge line for your answer

"The same gravitational condensation that lit up the stars also gathered the leftover nebular material into planetesimals — thus the origin of the universe flows seamlessly into the origin of our solar system and Earth." A sentence like this shows synthesis and earns marks.

08

Competing theories — know them to contrast

Knowing the rejected theories lets you write "unlike X, the Big Bang explains Y" — pure analytical value.

Big Bang vs its rivals
TheoryKey proponentCore ideaStatus
Big Bang / Expanding UniverseLemaître; Hubble (evidence) Universe began from a singularity ~13.7 bya and has expanded ever since. Accepted — backed by CMB, redshift.
Steady StateFred Hoyle (& others) Universe looks the same at all times/places; new matter is continuously created to keep density constant — no beginning, no end. Largely rejected — can't explain CMB.
Pulsating / Oscillating— Universe alternately expands & contracts in cycles (Bang → expansion → "Big Crunch" → next Bang). Speculative; not currently favoured.
💡
Irony worth quoting: Fred Hoyle championed the rival Steady State theory, yet it was he who coined the term "Big Bang" — originally mockingly.

Prelims quick-fire

🔴 Memorise verbatim

▸Big Bang = Expanding Universe Hypothesis — most accepted.
▸Age of universe ≈ 13.7 billion years.
▸Edwin Hubble (1920s) → proved expansion.
▸Light Year = distance (~9.46 trillion km), not time.
▸Nebula = birthplace of stars (gas + dust).
▸Star self-luminous (fusion); planet reflects light.
▸Sun = nearest star; Milky Way = our galaxy; Andromeda nearest major.
▸CMB radiation = strongest proof of Big Bang.
▸Redshift → galaxies moving away (Doppler).
▸Expansion = space between galaxies grows, not galaxies.
▸Steady State → Fred Hoyle (rejected).
▸First atoms in 3 min; transparent in 3 lakh yrs at 4,500 K.
▸Constellation = apparent pattern (Saptarishi, Orion).
▸Lemaître = proposer of the "primeval atom".

PYQ analysis — UPSC CSE (2013 onward)

◆ Straight talk before you memorise anything

Be honest with your time. UPSC has NOT set a single direct question purely on the "origin of the universe / Big Bang" — in Prelims or Mains — in the 2013–2025 window. The rare cosmology-flavoured questions are older (pre-2013) or sit in the adjacent Universe–Solar System cluster (nearest star, galaxies, space weather).

So treat this as a concept-clarity + fact-recall topic, not a heavy answer-writing topic. The payoff is indirect: it feeds Prelims one-liners and gives your origin-of-the-Earth Mains answers a confident opening.

Q1Statements

With reference to a proclamation of National Emergency, consider the following statements:

  1. It can be proclaimed only on the written recommendation of the Cabinet.
  2. It must be approved by both Houses within one month by a special majority.
  3. Once approved it may continue for a maximum of three years.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(b). Statements 1 and 2 are correct — both are 44th Amendment safeguards. Statement 3 is wrong: the three-year outer limit belongs to Article 356. A National Emergency renews every six months indefinitely. Note 10, Section 01.
Q2Negative

Which one of the following statements about Article 358 is NOT correct?

(b). (c) is NOT correct. The 44th Amendment confined Article 358 to Emergencies declared on war or external aggression — armed rebellion is excluded. Article 359 covers all three grounds. Note 10, Section 03.
Q3Statements

With reference to Articles 358 and 359, consider the following statements:

  1. Article 358 suspends the rights themselves while Article 359 suspends only the right to move a court.
  2. Article 359 requires a separate order of the President.
  3. Articles 20 and 21 can be suspended under Article 359 during a National Emergency.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a). Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — the 44th Amendment placed Articles 20 and 21 permanently beyond suspension, the direct legislative answer to ADM Jabalpur. Note 10, Section 03.
Q4How many

Consider the following consequences of a proclamation under Article 356:

  1. The State Legislative Assembly is suspended or dissolved.
  2. Parliament acquires power to legislate for the State.
  3. The President assumes the powers of the State High Court.
  4. Fundamental Rights in the State stand suspended.

How many of the above are correct?

(b). Only 1 and 2 follow. The President may never assume the powers of the State High Court, and only a National Emergency touches Fundamental Rights. Note 10, Section 04.
Q5Direct

The power of the Lok Sabha to force revocation of a National Emergency may be triggered by written notice of at least:

(b). One-tenth, a 44th Amendment safeguard. Do not confuse it with one-fourth (signing a presidential impeachment charge) or fifty members (admitting a no-confidence or adjournment motion). Note 10, Section 02.
Q6Pairs

Consider the following pairs:

  1. 38th Amendment — made an emergency proclamation immune from judicial review
  2. 42nd Amendment — permitted a National Emergency to be confined to a part of India
  3. 44th Amendment — replaced "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion"

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

(d). All three. The 38th and 42nd loosened the provisions; the 44th rebuilt them. If a safeguard in Part XVIII is undated, the 44th is the safest guess. Note 10, Section 01.
Q7Statements

With reference to the continuation of President's Rule beyond one year, consider the following statements:

  1. A National Emergency must be in operation in the whole of India or in that State.
  2. The Election Commission must certify that elections to the State Assembly cannot be held.
  3. Both conditions were introduced by the 44th Amendment.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(d). All three. Both conditions must be satisfied together, and both came in with the 44th Amendment. The absolute maximum remains three years. Note 10, Section 04.
Q8Negative

Which one of the following was NOT a holding in S.R. Bommai v. Union of India (1994)?

(b). (d) is NOT a holding. Article 356 permits action on the Governor's report "or otherwise" — a report has never been legally necessary. The other three are core Bommai holdings. Note 10, Section 04.
Q9Direct

Which Article empowers the President to hold that the government of a State cannot be carried on in accordance with the Constitution where the State fails to comply with a Union direction?

(a). Article 365 is the trigger; Article 356 is the instrument. Article 355 is the Union's duty to protect States against external aggression and internal disturbance. Note 10, Section 04.
Q10Statements

With reference to a Financial Emergency under Article 360, consider the following statements:

  1. It requires approval by both Houses within two months by a simple majority.
  2. Once approved it continues indefinitely without periodic renewal.
  3. The salaries of Supreme Court and High Court judges may be reduced while it is in force.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(d). All three are accurate. Article 360 is the only emergency needing no periodic renewal, and the express power to reduce judicial salaries is its most-asked feature. It has never been proclaimed. Note 10, Section 05.
Q11How many

Consider the following statements about emergencies in India:

  1. A National Emergency has been proclaimed three times.
  2. A Financial Emergency has never been proclaimed.
  3. President's Rule has been imposed more than a hundred times.

How many of the above are correct?

(d). All three. 1962, 1971 and 1975 for Article 352; zero for Article 360, not even during the 1991 balance-of-payments crisis; well over 100 for Article 356. Note 10, Section 01.
Q12Assertion

Statement-I: The 44th Amendment is the single most important amendment to Part XVIII.
Statement-II: It replaced "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion", required a written Cabinet recommendation, and protected Articles 20 and 21 from suspension.

Which one of the following is correct?

(a). Both correct, and the second explains the first — those are three of the six safeguards the 44th introduced. Note 10, Section 01.
Q13Statements

Consider the following statements regarding the effects of a National Emergency:

  1. Parliament may legislate on any subject in the State List.
  2. Such laws made by Parliament cease to operate six months after the Emergency ends.
  3. The State Legislative Assembly stands dissolved.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a). Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — dissolution of the Assembly belongs to Article 356. Under Article 352 the State legislature continues to exist and may still legislate. Note 10, Section 02.
Q14Direct

During a National Emergency, the term of the Lok Sabha may be extended by Parliament:

(d). One year at a time, with no cumulative limit while the Emergency lasts, but never beyond six months after it ceases. The 1976 extension used exactly this power. Note 10, Section 02.
Q15Negative

Which one of the following is NOT a consequence of a Financial Emergency?

(b). (c) is NOT correct. Automatic suspension of Article 19 belongs to Article 358, operating only during a National Emergency on war or external aggression. A Financial Emergency leaves Fundamental Rights untouched. Note 10, Section 05.
Q16Statements

Consider the following statements:

  1. ADM Jabalpur (1976) held that the right to move a court for habeas corpus stood suspended during an Emergency.
  2. That decision was formally overruled in Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (2017).

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(c). Both correct. The decision was 4:1 with Justice H.R. Khanna dissenting, and the nine-judge Puttaswamy bench formally overruled it forty-one years later. Note 10, Section 02.
Q17Statements

With reference to Article 164, consider the following statements:

  1. The Council of Ministers including the Chief Minister shall not exceed 15% of the total strength of the Legislative Assembly.
  2. The number of Ministers in a State shall in no case be less than twelve.
  3. A Minister who is not a member of the State legislature must become one within six months.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(d). All three. Article 164(1A) supplies both the ceiling and the floor of twelve — the floor has no Union equivalent — and 164(4) the six-month rule. Note 03, Section 01.
Q18Negative

Which one of the following statements about the State Council of Ministers is NOT correct?

(a). (c) is NOT correct. Article 164(2) runs to the Legislative Assembly alone, never to the Council — exactly as the Union Council answers only to the Lok Sabha. Note 03, Section 03.
Q19Pairs

Consider the following pairs of provision and subject:

  1. Article 163 — Council of Ministers to aid and advise the Governor
  2. Article 167 — duties of the Chief Minister towards the Governor
  3. Article 164(1B) — disqualification of a defector from ministerial office

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

(c). All three. Learn the Union–State pairs together: 163↔74, 167↔78, 164(1B)↔75(1B). Note 03, Section 04.
Q20Statements

Consider the following statements regarding the Chief Minister:

  1. The resignation or death of the Chief Minister dissolves the entire Council of Ministers.
  2. The Governor is bound by the Chief Minister's advice in recommending President's Rule.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a). Statement 1 is correct — any other Minister's death creates only a vacancy. Statement 2 is wrong: recommending President's Rule is a discretionary function under Article 163. Note 03, Section 02.
Q21How many

In how many of the following States is a Minister in charge of tribal welfare constitutionally required?

  1. Chhattisgarh
  2. Jharkhand
  3. Bihar
  4. Odisha

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

(c). The four named in the proviso to Article 164(1) are Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh and Odisha. Bihar is the trap — removed by the 94th Amendment, 2006. Note 03, Section 01.
Q22Assertion

Statement-I: The Governor's position differs from the President's despite near-identical wording on aid and advice.
Statement-II: Article 163 contains an express discretionary exception and, unlike Article 74, has never been amended.

Which one of the following is correct?

(a). Both correct and causally linked. Article 74 was amended by the 42nd and 44th; Article 163 never was. Note 03, Section 04.
Q23Direct

The oath of office to a State Minister is administered by:

(b). Article 164(3) — the Governor. Compare: the President administers the oath to Union Ministers, but the Chief Justice of India swears in the President. Note 03, Section 01.
Q24Statements

Consider the following statements:

  1. A State Minister may be a member of either House of the State legislature in a bicameral State.
  2. A State Minister may speak in both Houses but vote only in the House of which he is a member.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(c). Both correct — the State analogue of Article 88, which gives the same right at the Union level. Note 03, Section 01.
Q25Statements

With reference to Article 169, consider the following statements:

  1. Parliament may abolish a Legislative Council only if the State Assembly passes a resolution by a special majority.
  2. Such a law of Parliament is deemed to be an amendment under Article 368.
  3. Parliament is bound to act on a resolution passed by the State Assembly.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(a). Only statement 1. Article 169 expressly says such a law is not an Article 368 amendment, and Parliament is not obliged to act — Andhra Pradesh's 2020 resolution is still pending. Note 04, Section 02.
Q26Negative

Which one of the following statements about a State Legislative Council is NOT correct?

(b). (d) is NOT correct. There is no joint sitting at State level — Article 108 applies only to Parliament. The Assembly simply outlasts the Council after four months. Note 04, Section 04.
Q27How many

Consider the following States:

  1. Karnataka
  2. Telangana
  3. Odisha
  4. Bihar

How many of the above currently have a Legislative Council?

(c). The six bicameral States are Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana and Uttar Pradesh. Odisha does not have one — its Assembly passed a resolution, but Parliament has not enacted the law. Note 04, Section 02.
Q28Pairs

Consider the following pairs of category and share in a Legislative Council:

  1. Elected by members of local bodies — one-third
  2. Elected by graduates of three years' standing — one-twelfth
  3. Nominated by the Governor — one-sixth

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

(c). All three. The full Article 171 set: local bodies 1/3, MLAs 1/3, graduates 1/12, teachers 1/12, Governor's nominees 1/6 — they sum to 1. Note 04, Section 03.
Q29Statements

Consider the following statements about an ordinary Bill in a bicameral State legislature:

  1. A Bill originating in the Legislative Council and rejected by the Assembly comes to an end.
  2. The Council can delay a Bill passed by the Assembly for a maximum of four months.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(c). Both correct — three months on first passage plus one month on second. The asymmetry is the point: the Council delays, the Assembly decides. Note 04, Section 04.
Q30Direct

The maximum and minimum strength of a State Legislative Assembly under Article 170 is:

(d). Maximum 500, minimum 60 for the Assembly. The figure 40 is the minimum for a Legislative Council — swapping the two is the standard error. Note 04, Section 01.
Q31Statements

With reference to the nomination of members, consider the following statements:

  1. The Governor nominates members to a Legislative Council from persons with special knowledge of literature, science, art, co-operative movement and social service.
  2. The President nominates members to the Rajya Sabha from the same five fields.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a). Statement 1 is correct — five fields under Article 171(5). Statement 2 is wrong: Article 80(3) lists only four, omitting co-operative movement. That one word is the whole question. Note 04, Section 03.
Q32Direct

The minimum age for membership of a State Legislative Council is:

(a). Thirty, mirroring the Rajya Sabha. The Legislative Assembly requires 25, mirroring the Lok Sabha. Note 04, Section 03.
Q33Assertion

Statement-I: A State Legislative Council is a far weaker chamber than the Rajya Sabha.
Statement-II: A Legislative Council can be abolished by ordinary law on a resolution of the very Assembly it is meant to review.

Which one of the following is correct?

(a). Both correct and causally linked. A chamber the Assembly can vote out of existence was never going to be given a veto. Note 04, Section 04.
Q34Negative

Which one of the following is NOT a function in which the State Legislative Council participates?

(b). (c) is NOT correct. Where State ratification is required under Article 368, the resolution is passed by the Legislative Assembly alone. The Council has no role. Note 04, Section 04.
Q35Statements

Consider the following statements regarding parliamentary committees:

  1. A Select Committee on a Bill consists of members drawn from one House only.
  2. A Select Committee is a Standing Committee of that House.
  3. A Joint Committee on a Bill ceases to exist once it presents its report.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(b). Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong — a Select Committee is ad hoc. The two axes are independent: permanent/temporary is one, one-House/both is the other. Note 03, committee taxonomy.
Q36Statements

With reference to a censure motion, consider the following statements:

  1. It must state the reasons for its adoption.
  2. It can be moved against an individual Minister.
  3. If passed, the Council of Ministers must resign.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a). Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — a censure motion compels explanation, not resignation. Only a no-confidence motion brings the government down. Note 03, motions.
Q37Negative

Which one of the following statements about a Point of Order is NOT correct?

(b). (b) is NOT correct. A Point of Order is strictly procedural. To raise substance, the devices are a Short Duration Discussion or a Calling Attention motion. Note 03, motions.
Q38Pairs

Consider the following pairs of device and description:

  1. Kangaroo closure — only important clauses are debated and the rest are passed over
  2. Guillotine — undiscussed demands are put to vote together when the allotted time expires
  3. Half-an-Hour Discussion — raised on a matter that has already been the subject of a question

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

(c). All three. The four closures are simple, by compartments, kangaroo and guillotine. A Short Duration Discussion, unlike a Half-an-Hour Discussion, does not follow an earlier question. Note 03, motions.
Q39How many

Consider the following committees:

  1. Public Accounts Committee
  2. Estimates Committee
  3. Committee on Public Undertakings
  4. Committee of Parliament on Official Language

How many of the above include members of the Rajya Sabha?

(c). PAC (15+7), Public Undertakings (15+7) and Official Language (20+10) include Rajya Sabha members. The Estimates Committee is Lok Sabha only — 30 members. Note 03, committees.
Q40Direct

The Committee of Parliament on Official Language is constituted under:

(d). Article 344(4) — it is the only parliamentary committee created by the Constitution itself. Thirty members: 20 Lok Sabha, 10 Rajya Sabha; the Union Home Minister chairs by convention. Note 03, committees.
Q41Negative

Which one of the following statements about the Public Accounts Committee is NOT correct?

(b). (d) is NOT correct — Ministers cannot be members of the PAC, the Estimates Committee or the Committee on Public Undertakings. The Speaker does appoint the PAC chairman; the convention governs whom he appoints. Note 03, committees.
Q42Statements

Consider the following statements:

  1. An adjournment motion needs the support of 50 members for admission.
  2. The Rajya Sabha cannot use an adjournment motion.
  3. A no-confidence motion may be moved against an individual Minister.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(b). Statements 1 and 2 are correct — the Rajya Sabha is barred because the motion carries an element of censure. Statement 3 is wrong: a no-confidence motion lies against the government only. Note 03, motions.
Q43Direct

A Bill introduced by a member who is not a Minister requires advance notice of:

(d). One month for a Private Member's Bill, against seven days for a Government Bill. They are taken up only on allotted days, usually Friday afternoons. Note 02, legislative process.
Q44Statements

With reference to Zero Hour and Question Hour, consider the following statements:

  1. In the Lok Sabha, Question Hour is the first hour of a sitting and Zero Hour follows it.
  2. In the Rajya Sabha, Zero Hour is taken up at 11 a.m. and Question Hour at 12 noon.
  3. Zero Hour finds no mention in the Rules of Procedure.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(c). All three. The Rajya Sabha shifted Question Hour to noon in 2014 to protect it from disruption, so the order differs by House — a statement about "Parliament" without naming the House is incomplete. Note 03, Section 01.
Q45Statements

With reference to Cabinet Committees, consider the following statements:

  1. They are established by the Rules of Business framed under Article 77(3).
  2. Their membership is restricted to Cabinet Ministers alone.
  3. They are set up by the Prime Minister according to the needs of the time.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(c). Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong — non-Cabinet Ministers may be members. The word "alone" is what makes it false. Note 04, Section 01.
Q46Negative

Which one of the following is NOT ordinarily chaired by the Prime Minister?

(d). (c) is NOT. Parliamentary Affairs and Accommodation go to the Home Minister; Security, Economic Affairs, Appointments and Political Affairs to the Prime Minister. Note 04, Section 02.
Q47Statements

With reference to Parliamentary Forums, consider the following statements:

  1. They have a statutory basis under a law made by Parliament.
  2. The Speaker of the Lok Sabha is ex-officio President of every Forum without exception.
  3. A Forum may summon officials and report its findings to the House for action.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

(d). None. Forums are neither constitutional nor statutory; the Rajya Sabha Chairman presides over the Forum on Population and Public Health; and a Forum has no power to summon or report for action — that is a parliamentary committee. Note 04, Section 03.
Q48Direct

A Group of Ministers authorised to take a decision itself, rather than merely to recommend, is called:

(d). An Empowered GoM. An ordinary GoM only recommends; an ad hoc Cabinet Committee is commonly called a Group of Ministers. Note 04, Section 01.
Q49How many

Consider the following:

  1. Cabinet Committees
  2. Parliamentary Forums
  3. Departmentally Related Standing Committees
  4. NITI Aayog

How many of the above are mentioned in the Constitution?

(c). None. Cabinet Committees rest on Rules of Business under Art 77(3); Forums on the presiding officers' initiative; DRSCs on Arts 105 and 118 read with the Rules, not by name; NITI Aayog on an executive resolution of 2015. Note 04, Sections 01 and 03.
Q50Direct

The first Parliamentary Forum, constituted in 2005, dealt with:

(d). Water Conservation and Management, constituted by the Speaker on 12 August 2005. Each Forum has not more than 31 members — up to 21 from the Lok Sabha and 10 from the Rajya Sabha. Note 04, Section 03.

M1. "The emergency provisions of the Indian Constitution have been criticised as an invitation to dictatorship, yet the record suggests the remedy has come from amendment and adjudication rather than repeal." Critically examine. (15 marks · 250 words)
Skeleton: Ambedkar's unitary-in-emergency design and the Assembly's objection → the criticism evidenced by 1975–77 and ADM Jabalpur → the repair by the 44th Amendment (six safeguards) and by Bommai → what remains unfixed (125+ uses, late remedies, Sarkaria and Punchhi unimplemented) → verdict.
M2. "A State Legislative Council is a chamber of revision without the power to revise." Critically examine, and assess whether Article 169 should be used more readily. (15 marks · 250 words)
Skeleton: Art 169's reversible design → why it cannot revise (3+1 months, no joint sitting, Art 368 ratification by the Assembly alone) → the case for retention (Art 171's expertise and local-body representation) → reform of the electoral colleges rather than abolition by convenience → verdict.
M3. "The Chief Minister's position mirrors the Prime Minister's in form but not in security." Discuss with reference to Articles 163 and 164. (10 marks · 150 words)
Skeleton: the identical form (164↔75, 167↔78) → where it breaks (Art 163's discretionary carve-out, never amended, against a twice-amended Art 74) → why (Art 156, pleasure of the President) → verdict.

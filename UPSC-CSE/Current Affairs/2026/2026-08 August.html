🌱 What a topic card contains

Every card has three zones, and each serves a different paper.

Prelims — the facts. What · who · when · where · how much · confusable with. Terse, because every line should be capable of becoming a statement in an MCQ.

Static hook. The Article or Schedule entry a question will ride on. UPSC rarely asks a current-affairs fact naked; it asks the fact attached to a static provision, and this is where that attachment is named.

Mains — the argument. Why it matters, how it works, pros and cons side by side, and a way-forward line specific enough to lift straight into an answer.

One entity · one card · both papers
Subject-balance floor, checked every week: Economy ≥2 · Environment ≥1 · Sci-Tech ≥1 · Polity and schemes 2–4 · International 1–2 · Reports 1. Where a week's news cannot fill the environment or sci-tech slot, it is filled from the standing backlog of recurring entities rather than left empty — an empty slot compounds across twelve months into a blind spot.

RBI Monetary Policy — August 2026

Economy · GS-III · ★★★★★
Prelims · the facts
What
The Monetary Policy Committee left the repo rate unchanged at 5.25%.
Who
The MPC — 6 members: 3 from the RBI including the Governor as chair, 3 external members appointed by the Centre. The Governor holds a casting vote in a tie.
When
Met 3–5 August 2026. The MPC meets at least four times a year.
How much
Repo held at 5.25%. GDP growth forecast raised; inflation forecast lowered.
Context
Global growth projected at about 2.7–3.0% for 2026, below the pre-pandemic average.
Confusable
The reverse repo and the Standing Deposit Facility. The SDF replaced the reverse repo as the floor of the corridor in 2022.
Static hook

The MPC is a statutory body under the RBI Act, 1934 as amended by the Finance Act, 2016 — not constitutional. Its mandate is an inflation target of 4% ± 2%, set by the Central Government in consultation with the RBI every five years. A statement making the RBI the target-setter is wrong.

Mains · the argument

Why it matters. The repo rate is the single price that transmits monetary policy into the whole credit system. Holding it signals that the central bank judges inflation contained enough to prioritise growth, without declaring the fight won.

How it works. The MPC votes; minutes and each member's vote are published with a lag, which is the accountability mechanism that replaced the older Governor-alone model.

Pros
  • Rate stability gives firms a predictable planning horizon for investment.
  • Raising the growth forecast while lowering the inflation forecast is a genuinely favourable combination, and rare.
  • The statutory framework depersonalises the decision and makes it reviewable.
Cons
  • Transmission to lending rates remains slow and uneven across bank categories.
  • A resilient domestic economy sits alongside a vulnerable external sector — energy prices and capital flows are outside the MPC's reach.
  • Holding rates does nothing for credit availability where the constraint is risk aversion rather than price.
Way forward — pair rate stability with transmission monitoring and external-sector buffers, since the binding constraint in 2026 is external, not domestic.

Census of India 2027

Polity / Governance · GS-II · ★★★★★
Prelims · the facts
What
The 16th Census of India, and the first conducted digitally.
Who
Conducted by the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India, under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
When
Phase I — Houselisting and Housing Census, April–September 2026. Phase II — Population Enumeration, February 2027.
Reference date
00:00 hours, 1 March 2027 — but 00:00 hours, 1 October 2026 for Ladakh and the snow-bound areas of Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand. Two reference dates: that is the question.
How much
Approved at ₹11,718.24 crore.
Two firsts
First digital census, data submitted by enumerators through a mobile app; and the first with an option of self-enumeration.
Caste
Caste enumeration included, in Phase II. Decided by the Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs on 30 April 2025.
Confusable
The National Population Register and the NRC — distinct exercises with different legal bases. The Census is under the Census Act, 1948; the NPR under the Citizenship Rules, 2003.
Static hook

Entry 69 of the Union List — census is exclusively a Union subject, which is why no State can conduct its own official census. It also drives Article 82 readjustment of Lok Sabha seats: the delimitation freeze under the 84th Amendment runs to the first census after 2026, and the 106th Amendment women's reservation takes effect only after the delimitation following that census. This single linkage connects Census 2027 to three separate Polity topics.

Mains · the argument

Why it matters. The 2027 census unlocks a chain of constitutional consequences that have been frozen since 1976 — seat readjustment, women's reservation, and the federal arithmetic of representation between demographically divergent States.

How it works. Two phases separated by nearly a year, with a common reference moment so that a population count taken over weeks is comparable to a single instant.

Pros
  • Digital collection with a mobile app cuts the transcription errors that plagued paper schedules.
  • Self-enumeration reduces enumerator burden and improves coverage of hard-to-reach urban households.
  • Caste enumeration supplies the empirical base that reservation policy has lacked since 1931.
Cons
  • Digital-only capture risks under-counting households with poor connectivity or device access.
  • Caste enumeration is contested on the ground that classification can harden identities it merely intends to measure.
  • Delayed from 2021, the data gap has already distorted a decade of welfare targeting and seat allocation.
Way forward — publish the methodology and micro-data protocols in advance, so the count is trusted before it is used to redraw seats and reservations.

Semicon 2.0 — carried forward

Sci-Tech / Economy · GS-III · ★★★★★
Prelims · the facts
What
Programme to build India's semiconductor design and manufacturing ecosystem.
Who
Ministry of Electronics and IT; approved by the Union Cabinet.
When
15 July 2026 (PIB PRID 2284784). Succeeds Semicon 1.0.
How much
₹1,27,500 crore.
Six pillars
Design · Machines & Materials · Fabs · ATMP/OSAT · R&D · Talent. Six is the count a question uses.
The detail
The Design pillar cites 105 startups already developing chips.
Confusable
The Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme — same ministry, same month, ₹62,500 crore, but handsets rather than chips.
Static hook

Entry 52 of the Union List — industries Parliament declares expedient in the public interest. Industry is otherwise Entry 24 of the State List, so Entry 52 is what makes a national semiconductor policy competent.

Mains · the argument

Why it matters. Semiconductors are a strategic input to defence, telecom, automotive and payments. Post-2020 disruptions exposed how concentrated the global supply chain is.

How it works. ATMP/OSAT — assembly, testing, marking and packaging — is the labour-intensive, lower-capital end of the chain, and the realistic entry point before leading-edge fabrication.

Pros
  • India already has a design base — 105 startups — so the front-end capability exists.
  • Six pillars cover the whole value chain rather than subsidising fabs alone.
  • ATMP is a segment where India can compete on cost immediately.
Cons
  • Leading-edge fabs are capital-hungry and cyclical; subsidy-led entry can build capacity without competitiveness.
  • The binding constraint is manufacturing talent, which cannot be bought with capex.
  • Earlier incentive schemes disbursed against announced investment rather than output.
Way forward — sequence talent and ATMP first, leading-edge fabs later, and tie disbursement to output milestones rather than announced investment.

Vibrant Villages Programme — border connectivity

Security / Governance · GS-III · ★★★★☆
Prelims · the facts
What
Comprehensive development of villages along the northern border.
Who
Ministry of Home Affairs; roads largely executed by the Border Roads Organisation under the Ministry of Defence.
Where
Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh and Ladakh — four States and one UT. The five-name list is the question.
How much
2,967 villages across 46 blocks in 19 districts. Phase 1 covers about 1.42 lakh people in 662 villages.
Roads
All villages within a 5 km radius of the international border now have all-weather roads; the Arunachal Frontier Highway runs 1,859 km.
Confusable
BADP — Border Area Development Programme, all borders, older framework.
Static hook

Defence is Entry 1 of the Union List; border-area development sits at the intersection of security and Centre–State relations. Compare the Border Area Development Programme, which covers all international borders — VVP is northern-border specific.

Mains · the argument

Why it matters. Depopulation of border villages creates a security vacuum. The programme treats settlement itself as strategic infrastructure — "first villages, not last villages" is the reframing.

How it works. Convergence model: existing schemes are prioritised in identified villages rather than a new funding line for every sector.

Pros
  • Reverses out-migration by making border habitation viable.
  • A settled population is an intelligence and surveillance asset in difficult terrain.
  • Connectivity has dual civilian and military use.
Cons
  • Convergence depends on State delivery capacity, which is thin in exactly these districts.
  • Terrain and short working seasons make road timelines slip.
  • Focus on the northern border leaves other sensitive frontiers on the older BADP framework.
Way forward — publish village-level progress dashboards so convergence is auditable, and extend the model to other frontiers once proven.

Coastal and marine conservation — standing backlog card

Environment · GS-III · ★★★★☆
Prelims · the facts
What
The recurring environment entities behind India's coastal and marine questions.
CRZ
Coastal Regulation Zone notifications issued under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. CRZ-I is ecologically sensitive; CRZ-IV is the water area up to the territorial limit.
Who enforces
National Coastal Zone Management Authority at the Centre, and State CZMAs.
Ramsar
India's wetland count under the Ramsar Convention (1971, Iran) has risen sharply; the convention's theme is "wise use". Montreux Record lists sites with ecological change — India has Keoladeo and Loktak on it.
Mangroves
MISHTI — Mangrove Initiative for Shoreline Habitats and Tangible Incomes, announced in the 2023-24 Budget.
Confusable
Ramsar (wetlands, 1971) versus CITES (trade in species, 1973) versus the Bonn Convention (migratory species, 1979). Three conventions, three subjects, frequently swapped.
Static hook

Forests and protection of wild animals and birds are Entry 17A and 17B of the Concurrent List, moved there by the 42nd Amendment. Environmental protection also draws on Article 48A (DPSP) and Article 51A(g) (Fundamental Duty) — the pair that appears in almost every environment-Polity linkage question.

Mains · the argument

Why it matters. Environment carries 13–15% of Prelims, and coastal and wetland entities recur year after year regardless of what happened that week. This card exists because the week's news did not fill the environment slot — and per the method, an empty slot is not acceptable.

Pros
  • Ramsar designation brings international reporting discipline without ceding sovereignty.
  • CRZ zoning gives a legal basis to resist unplanned coastal construction.
  • Mangroves deliver carbon sequestration and storm-surge protection together.
Cons
  • CRZ notifications have been repeatedly diluted, weakening the zoning's protective intent.
  • Ramsar listing carries no enforcement teeth; the Montreux Record is advisory.
  • State CZMAs are under-staffed relative to the coastline they police.
Way forward — strengthen State CZMA capacity and make CRZ clearances publicly traceable, so zoning is enforced rather than merely declared.

Questions — August 2026

Linkage · Statements

With reference to the Monetary Policy Committee, consider the following statements:

  1. It is a statutory body constituted under the RBI Act, 1934 as amended in 2016.
  2. The inflation target is set by the Reserve Bank of India.
  3. The Governor of the RBI has a casting vote in the event of a tie.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

  • (a) Only one
  • (b) Only two
  • (c) All three
  • (d) None
Answer: (b) — only two
  • 1 and 3 are correct. The MPC has six members — three from the RBI, three external — and the Governor chairs with a casting vote.
  • 2 is wrong. The target of 4% ± 2% is set by the Central Government in consultation with the RBI, every five years. Making the RBI the target-setter is the standard trap.
  • The MPC is statutory, not constitutional — the same category test that separates UPSC from SSC and NTA.
Linkage · Direct

Census is a subject in the:

  • (a) State List
  • (b) Concurrent List
  • (c) Union List
  • (d) Residuary powers
Answer: (c) — Union List
  • Entry 69 of the Union List. This is why no State can conduct an official census of its own.
  • It links onward to Article 82 readjustment, the 84th Amendment freeze running to the first census after 2026, and the 106th Amendment women's reservation, which takes effect only after the delimitation following that census.
  • One entry, three Polity topics — this is what a linkage question is built to reward.
Linkage · Statements

Consider the following statements regarding Census 2027:

  1. It will be the first census in India conducted digitally.
  2. The reference date is the same for the entire country.
  3. Caste enumeration will be carried out in the second phase.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

  • (a) 1 and 2 only
  • (b) 1 and 3 only
  • (c) 2 and 3 only
  • (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b) — 1 and 3 only
  • 1 and 3 are correct. First digital census, with self-enumeration also offered for the first time; caste enumeration sits in Phase II.
  • 2 is wrong — there are two reference dates. 00:00 hrs, 1 March 2027 generally, but 00:00 hrs, 1 October 2026 for Ladakh and the snow-bound areas of J&K, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand.
  • Whenever a statement says "the same for the entire country", look for the exception.
Linkage · Direct

Parliament's competence to frame a national semiconductor policy, notwithstanding that industry is a State List subject, rests on:

  • (a) Article 249
  • (b) Entry 52 of the Union List
  • (c) Article 282
  • (d) Article 254
Answer: (b) — Entry 52 of the Union List
  • Industry is Entry 24 of the State List, but Entry 52 covers industries Parliament declares by law to be expedient in the public interest.
  • (c) Article 282 is the grant-making power — the right answer for a scheme in a State subject such as PM-KISAN, but not for legislative competence.
  • Learn the pair: Entry 52 for competence, Article 282 for money.
Linkage · How many

Consider the following constitutional provisions:

  1. Article 48A
  2. Article 51A(g)
  3. Entry 17A of the Concurrent List

How many of the above relate to protection of the environment or forests?

  • (a) Only one
  • (b) Only two
  • (c) All three
  • (d) None
Answer: (c) — all three
  • Article 48A is the DPSP duty on the State to protect the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife; Article 51A(g) is the matching Fundamental Duty on citizens. Both were inserted by the 42nd Amendment.
  • Entry 17A (forests) and 17B (protection of wild animals and birds) were moved from the State List to the Concurrent List by the same amendment.
  • That 42nd Amendment cluster is the standard environment–Polity linkage, and it recurs every year.
Practice · How many

Consider the following States and Union Territories:

  1. Sikkim
  2. Himachal Pradesh
  3. Nagaland
  4. Ladakh

How many of the above are covered by the Vibrant Villages Programme?

  • (a) Only two
  • (b) Only three
  • (c) All four
  • (d) Only one
Answer: (b) — only three
  • The programme covers Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh and Ladakh — four States and one UT, all on the northern border.
  • Nagaland is the trap — it borders Myanmar, not China, so it falls outside this programme.
  • For all international borders, the older Border Area Development Programme applies.
Practice · Pairs

Consider the following pairs of convention and subject:

  1. Ramsar Convention — wetlands
  2. CITES — trade in endangered species
  3. Bonn Convention — migratory species

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

  • (a) None
  • (b) Only one
  • (c) Only two
  • (d) All three
Answer: (d) — all three
  • All correct. Ramsar 1971 (Iran), CITES 1973, Bonn 1979 — three conventions routinely swapped in pairs questions.
  • Ramsar's operating principle is "wise use"; the Montreux Record lists sites undergoing ecological change, and India has Keoladeo and Loktak on it.
  • Note the ladder runs None → All three, so the all-correct option sits at (d).
Practice · Negative

Which one of the following statements is NOT correct?

  • (a) The repo rate was left unchanged at 5.25% in August 2026
  • (b) Census 2027 was approved at a cost of about ₹11,718 crore
  • (c) Semicon 2.0 rests on four pillars
  • (d) The Arunachal Frontier Highway is about 1,859 km long
Answer: (c) is NOT correct
  • Semicon 2.0 has six pillars — Design, Machines & Materials, Fabs, ATMP/OSAT, R&D and Talent.
  • (a), (b) and (d) are all accurate as stated.
  • Circle the NOT, test each option normally, flip at the end.

Cross-week themes

The reason a month exists as a unit: patterns that only appear when weeks are read together.

✚
Theme — the Seventh Schedule is doing most of the work. Three of Week 1's five cards turn on a List entry: Census on Entry 69, Union List; semiconductors on Entry 52, Union List against Entry 24 State List; environment on Entries 17A and 17B, moved to the Concurrent List by the 42nd Amendment. If you learn one static thing alongside current affairs this month, learn the Seventh Schedule entries that keep recurring.
✚
Theme — competence versus money. Two different constitutional routes keep appearing and are routinely confused. Entry 52 gives Parliament power to legislate on an industry otherwise left to States. Article 282 gives the Union power to spend on a State subject. Semicon uses the first; PM-KISAN and Khelo India use the second.
!
Theme — statutory is not constitutional. The MPC is statutory (RBI Act 1934, amended 2016). The Registrar General is an office under the MHA. The UPSC is constitutional. NITI Aayog is executive. Four categories, and the boundary between them is one of the three most reliable linkage hooks in the paper.
